Data Collected

The app works without a user account. The following data may be stored locally on your device (browser):

  • Preferences: theme (light/dark), selected island (Martinique / Guadeloupe), favorites (beach list), language preference (fr/en).
  • Progress Data: XP points, badges, beach consultation statistics, stored only on your device.
  • Location: if you enable geolocation, the position is used only to sort beaches by distance and show the nearest beach; it is never sent to our servers.

We do not sell your data. No personal data (name, email, phone) is required to use the service.

Cookies & Local Storage

The site uses browser local storage to remember your settings and favorites. No advertising or third-party tracking cookies are placed by us. Any cookies present are from third-party services (maps, weather) when you click external links.

Technical Data & Analytics

The host records technical data (IP address, browser type, pages viewed) to ensure operation and security. We use Google Analytics for audience measurement (anonymous statistics, truncated IP, page views) to improve the service; this data is neither sold nor used for advertising. All processing complies with applicable regulations.

Your Rights

You can at any time delete locally stored data by clearing the site storage in your browser settings. For any questions about your personal data or to exercise your rights (access, rectification, opposition), contact us at alerte@sargasses-martinique.com.

Updates

This policy may be updated. The last update date will be indicated at the bottom of the page if necessary.
