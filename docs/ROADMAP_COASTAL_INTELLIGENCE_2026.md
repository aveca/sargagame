# Sargagame — Coastal Decision Intelligence Roadmap (2026)

## Strategic position
Become a trusted decision product for beaches and coastal operators, not just a map or generic AI chatbot. Help people decide where to go, when to go and how to prepare, using current evidence.

## Product loop: SEE → DECIDE → GO → PROTECT
- **SEE:** observed conditions, freshness and source provenance.
- **DECIDE:** beach-specific recommendation with confidence and uncertainty.
- **GO:** help travelers/operators take a concrete action.
- **PROTECT:** useful alerts and operational guidance when conditions change.

## Priorities
### P0 — Trust and production evidence
- Keep Copernicus/ERDDAP as the scientific source of truth.
- Show observation time, forecast horizon, confidence and stale-data warnings.
- Test pipeline failure and region isolation.
- Never let paid placement alter scientific beach status.
- Verify production endpoints, payment flow and scheduled jobs with recorded evidence.

### P1 — Prove willingness to pay
- Interview hotels, tour operators and coastal businesses.
- Run small B2B pilots with named buyers, baseline, success metric and renewal decision.
- Measure activation, repeat usage, acted-on alerts, paid conversion and retention.
- Fix measured funnel friction before buying traffic or adding features.
- Trigger offers from user intent and value, not merely elapsed time.

### P2 — Decision-grade intelligence
- Explain the basis of each recommendation and its uncertainty.
- Compare beaches without implying unsupported certainty.
- Send operational alerts only when thresholds and data quality justify them.
- Offer a documented API only after freshness, availability and terms are reliable.

### P3 — Expand selectively
- Add regions only after local data quality and support economics are validated.
- Explore erosion, flooding or water quality only with qualified data and committed buyers.
- Keep the core beach decision workflow focused.

## Commercial hypotheses to test
- Free: current-day status with source/freshness indicators.
- Consumer paid: multi-day forecast, saved beaches and useful alerts.
- B2B: multi-location monitoring, team alerts, operational reports and API access when reliable.
These are hypotheses, not validated revenue claims; test pricing with customers.

## Metrics
Data freshness and pipeline success; forecast calibration; time to first useful decision; return usage; alert engagement; paywall→checkout and checkout→confirmed payment; B2B pilot activation/renewal; incidents and recovery time.

## 90-day experiment
1. Baseline forecast reliability and the complete paid funnel.
2. Select one B2B segment and recruit pilot customers.
3. Ship only changes tied to decision quality or measured conversion friction.
4. Record timestamp, commit, endpoint and outcome for production validation.
5. Review evidence before adding regions or data products.

## Guardrails
No fabricated observations, unsupported forecast certainty, payment success without provider confirmation, production-ready claims without live evidence, or commercial influence on scientific status.

**Status:** proposed roadmap; does not certify production health, forecast accuracy or revenue.
